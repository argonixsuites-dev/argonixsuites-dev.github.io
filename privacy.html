2. Information That Stays on Your Device

All document scanning, viewing, editing, compressing, merging, and other processing happens locally on your device. We do not receive, store, view, or have access to:

  • Your documents, PDFs, scans, or images
  • Your file names or folder contents
  • Camera images or the camera feed
  • Anything you type or annotate inside the Apps

Files you create are stored in the App's local storage or in locations on your device that you choose. Because we never receive these files, we cannot access, recover, or delete them for you. They are controlled entirely by you and your device.

Device Permissions

The Apps may ask for the following permissions. You can grant or revoke them at any time in your device settings. Some features may not work without them.

  • Camera (ArgonixPDF): Used to scan documents. Captured images are processed and saved on your device only. They are never transmitted to us or anyone else.
  • Photos / Media Library: Used only if you choose to import an image or save output to your photo library.
  • Files / Storage: Used to open, read, edit, and save PDFs and images that you select.
  • Internet access (ArgonixPDF): Used only to process and verify in-app purchases, as described in Section 3. Document features do not need an internet connection.

3. Information Collected for In-App Purchases (ArgonixPDF only)

ArgonixPDF offers an optional one-time purchase ("ArgonixPDF Pro"). To process, verify, and restore that purchase, limited data is handled by the following parties.

Apple and Google (Payment Processing)

All payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your name, payment card details, billing address, or store account email. Apple and Google handle your payment information under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

RevenueCat (Purchase Verification)

We use RevenueCat, Inc. as a service provider to validate purchase receipts and manage access to Pro features, including restoring your purchase on a new device. When ArgonixPDF checks your purchase status, RevenueCat may collect and process:

  • Purchase history: the products purchased, transaction identifiers, purchase dates, and the store receipt or purchase token issued by Apple or Google
  • An anonymous app user identifier: a random ID generated by RevenueCat that is not linked to your name, email, or other contact details
  • Basic device and app information: such as device model, operating system version, app version, and the last time the App checked purchase status
  • IP address and approximate country or region: derived from the network connection, used for purchase processing, fraud prevention, and pricing and currency purposes

RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf and only to provide its services to us. RevenueCat does not receive your documents, files, camera images, name, or email address from the Apps. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

If you never start or make a purchase, ArgonixPDF may still contact RevenueCat to check whether Pro is active on your store account. This check involves only the categories of data listed above.

ArgonixReader

ArgonixReader is free, has no in-app purchases, and does not send any data off your device.

4. How We Use Information

We use the limited purchase-verification information described in Section 3 only to:

  • Confirm and unlock ArgonixPDF Pro features
  • Restore your purchase on new or reinstalled devices
  • Prevent fraud and abuse of purchases
  • Comply with legal obligations, such as tax and accounting record-keeping
  • View aggregated, non-identifying purchase totals (for example, the number of Pro purchases) to understand how our business is doing

We do not use any information for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Sharing of Information

We share the limited information described in Section 3 only:

  • With service providers: Apple, Google, and RevenueCat, solely to process and verify purchases
  • For legal reasons: when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or when needed to protect our rights, our users, or the public
  • In a business transfer: if ArgonixSuites is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets. In that case, this Privacy Policy will continue to apply to information collected before the transfer.

6. Data Retention

  • Your documents and files: These are never received by us, so we do not retain them. They remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • Purchase-verification data: RevenueCat keeps this data for as long as needed to provide purchase verification and restoration, and as required for legal, tax, and accounting purposes. You can request deletion as described in Section 8.

7. Data Security

Purchase-verification data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS). Because your documents never leave your device, their security also depends on your device's own protections, such as your passcode, biometric lock, and device encryption. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Wherever you live, you may:

  • Ask what information about you we or our service providers hold
  • Ask for that information to be deleted
  • Ask for a correction of inaccurate information
  • Delete all on-device data at any time by deleting your files, clearing the App's data, or uninstalling the App

To make a request, email argonixsuites.dev@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." Because we don't use accounts, please include the Purchase ID shown in ArgonixPDF under Settings → About so we can find the related purchase records. We will respond within 45 days. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

Note: Deleting your purchase-verification data may prevent ArgonixPDF from restoring your Pro purchase automatically. You can usually still restore it through "Restore Purchases" using the same Apple or Google account.
