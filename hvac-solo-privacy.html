This policy explains what information HVAC-Solo handles, where it is kept and who it is shared with. In short: your business records live on your device, and nothing leaves it unless you send it.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

HVAC-Solo keeps your business records in a local SQLite database on this device: your company profile, customer contact records (names, phone numbers, email and service addresses, gate codes, notes), service locations, contacts, equipment, jobs, estimates, invoices, signatures, price books, memberships and field forms. Photos, videos and documents you attach are copied into the app's private storage. We do not operate servers that receive, host or back up this information, and we cannot see it.

2. Device Permissions

Camera: used only when you choose to take a photo or video for a job, estimate, customer, price-book item or your logo. Photos, media and files: used only when you choose to attach an existing photo, video or document. Recording video with the camera may also use the microphone. You can deny or revoke any permission in your device settings; the related feature simply stops working.

3. When Information Leaves Your Device

Information leaves the device only when you act: sending an estimate, invoice, report or message, sharing a PDF, exporting a backup, or opening a review link. By default, emails and texts are handed to your device's own email, SMS or share apps, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Optional Email Relays You Configure

If you choose to set up an email relay in Settings (Google Apps Script, Brevo, Resend, SendGrid or EmailJS), messages you send, including the recipient's name and email address, the message text and any attached PDF, are transmitted to that provider using the account and API key you supply. You choose the provider and agree to its terms and privacy policy. Relays are off unless you configure one.

5. Your Customers' Information

You are responsible for collecting and using your customers' information lawfully, including getting any consent required before sending them emails or text messages.

6. What We Do Not Collect

HVAC-Solo has no user accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share personal information. Small app settings (such as your free-trial start date) are stored on the device only. Subscription purchases, when available, are processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy.

7. Security, Retention & Deletion

Your records stay on the device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Uninstalling, or clearing the app's storage, permanently deletes them. Backup files you export contain your business records (not passwords or API keys): store them somewhere safe. Protect your device with a screen lock.

8. Children & Changes

HVAC-Solo is a business tool for adults and is not directed to children under 13. If this policy changes materially, the app will ask you to review and accept it again, and the updated version will be posted on this page.
